# Company, Contact & Deal Setup

## Objective

Establish the core CRM relationship between a company, its primary contact, and a sales opportunity.

## Company

**Zenith Bank Plc**

- Industry: Banking
- Country: Nigeria

## Contact

**Ada Okafor**

- Job Title: Procurement Manager
- Associated with Zenith Bank Plc

## Deal

**Corporate Catering – Zenith Bank**

- Amount: ₦450,000
- Stage: Appointment Scheduled
- Pipeline: Sales Pipeline

## Business Value

Linking companies, contacts, and deals provides a complete view of customer relationships, improves collaboration, and enables accurate sales tracking.

## Lessons Learned

- Deals should always be associated with a company whenever possible.
- Contacts should be linked to both the company and the opportunity.
- Proper associations improve reporting and customer visibility.
