# Sales Pipeline Dashboard

## Overview

A sales dashboard was created in HubSpot to provide Gochelicious with a centralized view of deal activity, pipeline performance, revenue forecasting, and sales progress across the catering sales process.

## Dashboard Reports

The dashboard includes the following reports:

- Deals Open by Current Stage
- Deal Revenue Forecast by Owner with Stage Breakdown
- Closed Revenue by Month with Deal Total and Closed Revenue Breakdown
- Deals in Sales Pipeline Stages by Representative
- Deal Revenue Forecast by Stage

## Business Purpose

The dashboard enables management to monitor:

- The number of deals in each pipeline stage
- The total value of opportunities across the pipeline
- Forecasted revenue based on deal progression
- Closed revenue performance over time
- Sales activity across the catering sales process

## Configuration Notes

The dashboard was created using HubSpot's reporting tools and customized to display key sales metrics relevant to Gochelicious Premium Catering.

During implementation, report filters were reviewed and adjusted to ensure that all sample deals appeared correctly across the various pipeline stages.

The completed dashboard displays activity across the following stages:

- New Inquiry
- Consultation Scheduled
- Quotation Sent
- Negotiation
- Deposit Received
- Closed Won

## Business Benefits

- Provides a centralized view of the sales pipeline
- Improves visibility into deal progression
- Supports revenue forecasting
- Helps identify sales bottlenecks
- Enables better business decision-making through reporting
