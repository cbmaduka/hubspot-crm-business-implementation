# Custom Reports

## Overview

Custom reports were created in HubSpot to provide actionable insights into the sales pipeline, revenue forecasting, and deal performance for Gochelicious Premium Catering.

## Reports Created

| Report | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Deals Open by Current Stage | Tracks the number of deals currently in each sales pipeline stage. |
| Deal Revenue Forecast by Owner with Stage Breakdown | Forecasts expected revenue grouped by deal owner and pipeline stage. |
| Deal Revenue Forecast by Stage | Displays projected revenue across the different sales stages. |
| Deals in Sales Pipeline Stages by Rep | Measures the number of deals assigned to each sales representative by stage. |
| Closed Revenue by Month with Deal Total and Closed Revenue Breakdown | Monitors monthly closed revenue trends and overall deal performance. |

## Business Benefits

- Improved visibility into pipeline performance.
- Supports revenue forecasting and sales planning.
- Enables monitoring of sales representative performance.
- Identifies bottlenecks within the sales pipeline.
- Provides management with data-driven decision-making.

## Lessons Learned

Building custom reports transforms CRM data into meaningful business insights. Well-designed reports enable stakeholders to monitor performance, forecast revenue, and make informed operational decisions.
