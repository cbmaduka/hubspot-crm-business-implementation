# Business Requirements

## Client

Gochelicious Premium Catering

## Industry

Hospitality & Catering Services

## Project Objective

Implement HubSpot CRM to centralize customer information, improve lead management, streamline the sales process, automate follow-ups, and provide visibility into business performance.

## Current Challenges

- Customer information is stored across multiple channels.
- Sales follow-ups are inconsistent.
- Quotations are tracked manually.
- Deposits are difficult to monitor.
- There is no standardized sales process.
- Reporting is limited.

## Project Goals

- Centralize customer records.
- Standardize the sales pipeline.
- Track enquiries from first contact to completed event.
- Improve customer follow-up.
- Automate repetitive tasks where possible.
- Generate meaningful sales reports.

## Success Criteria

- Every enquiry is recorded in HubSpot.
- Every deal follows a defined pipeline.
- Sales activities are tracked.
- Customer communication is documented.
- Management can monitor pipeline performance using dashboards.
