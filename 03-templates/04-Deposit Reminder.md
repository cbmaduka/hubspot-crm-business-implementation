# Deposit Reminder

## Purpose

This email template reminds clients to make the required deposit to secure their catering booking.

---

## Email Subject

Deposit Reminder – Secure Your Catering Booking

---

## Email Body

Hello {{First Name}},

We hope you're doing well.

This is a friendly reminder that your event booking is currently awaiting the required deposit to secure your reservation.

### Booking Summary

- **Event Date:** {{Event Date}}
- **Venue:** {{Venue}}
- **Service Package:** {{Service Package}}

Once your deposit has been received, your booking will be confirmed and our operations team will begin preparing for your event.

If you have already made payment, kindly disregard this message. Otherwise, please let us know if you require our payment details or have any questions.

Thank you, and we look forward to serving you.

Kind regards,

**Gochelicious Sales Team**

---

## CRM Usage

- Sent to clients whose quotations have been accepted but whose deposits are still outstanding.
- Encourages timely payment to secure event bookings.
- Helps reduce abandoned opportunities.

---

## Lesson Learned

Timely payment reminders improve booking conversion rates, reduce scheduling uncertainty, and help ensure that event dates are reserved only after the required deposit has been received.
