# Initial Catering Inquiry

## Purpose

This email template is used to acknowledge new catering inquiries and begin the client engagement process.

---

## Email Subject

Thank you for contacting Gochelicious Catering

---

## Email Body

Hello {{First Name}},

Thank you for reaching out to Gochelicious.

We appreciate your interest in our catering services and would be delighted to support your upcoming event.

To prepare an accurate quotation, kindly provide the following information:

- Event date
- Event location
- Type of event
- Estimated number of guests
- Preferred catering package
- Dietary requirements (if any)

Once we receive these details, our team will prepare a customized proposal and contact you promptly.

We look forward to making your event memorable.

Kind regards,

**Gochelicious Sales Team**

---

## CRM Usage

- Sent immediately after a new inquiry is received.
- Helps standardize client communication.
- Ensures sales representatives collect all information required before preparing a quotation.

---

## Lesson Learned

Using standardized response templates improves response time, ensures consistent communication, and reduces the risk of missing important event details during lead qualification.
