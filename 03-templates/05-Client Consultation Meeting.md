# Client Consultation Meeting

## Purpose

This meeting template helps sales representatives gather all the information required before preparing a catering proposal.

---

## Meeting Agenda

### Client Information

- Client Name:
- Company (if applicable):
- Phone Number:
- Email Address:

### Event Details

- Event Type:
- Event Date:
- Event Time:
- Venue:
- Estimated Guest Count:

### Catering Requirements

- Preferred Menu
- Service Style (Buffet, Plated, Cocktail, etc.)
- Dietary Requirements
- Beverage Requirements

### Budget

- Estimated Budget
- Special Requests

### Next Steps

- Prepare quotation
- Confirm menu options
- Schedule follow-up
- Send proposal

---

## CRM Usage

- Used during discovery meetings with prospective clients.
- Ensures all event requirements are captured before creating a quotation.
- Provides a standardized process for gathering customer information.

---

## Lesson Learned

Using a structured meeting template improves consistency, reduces the risk of missing important client requirements, and helps deliver more accurate catering proposals.
