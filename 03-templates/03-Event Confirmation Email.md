# Event Confirmation Email

## Purpose

This email template confirms a client's catering booking after the quotation has been accepted and the required deposit has been received.

---

## Email Subject

Your Catering Event Has Been Confirmed

---

## Email Body

Hello {{First Name}},

Thank you for choosing Gochelicious.

We're pleased to confirm your catering booking for your upcoming event.

### Event Summary

- **Event Date:** {{Event Date}}
- **Venue:** {{Venue}}
- **Guest Count:** {{Number of Guests}}
- **Service Package:** {{Service Package}}

Our team will contact you before the event to confirm final logistics and ensure everything is ready for a seamless experience.

Thank you for trusting us with your event. We look forward to serving you.

Kind regards,

**Gochelicious Sales Team**

---

## CRM Usage

- Sent automatically or manually after the client's booking has been confirmed.
- Provides reassurance that the event has been successfully scheduled.
- Reduces uncertainty by summarizing key booking details.

---

## Lesson Learned

Confirmation emails improve customer confidence, reduce misunderstandings, and provide a documented summary of the agreed event details.
