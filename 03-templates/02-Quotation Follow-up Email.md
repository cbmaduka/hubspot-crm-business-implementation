# Quotation Follow-up Email

## Purpose

This email template is used to follow up with prospective clients after a catering quotation has been sent.

---

## Email Subject

Following Up on Your Catering Proposal

---

## Email Body

Hello {{First Name}},

I hope you're doing well.

I'm following up regarding the catering proposal we shared for your upcoming event.

Please let us know if you have any questions or would like to make adjustments to the menu, guest count, or service package. We're happy to tailor the proposal to meet your needs.

If you're ready to proceed, we'll prepare the event for booking and share the next steps.

We look forward to serving you.

Kind regards,

**Gochelicious Sales Team**

---

## CRM Usage

- Sent 2–3 days after a quotation has been delivered.
- Encourages client engagement without being intrusive.
- Helps move opportunities through the sales pipeline.

---

## Lesson Learned

Consistent follow-up increases conversion rates by keeping potential clients engaged and demonstrating responsiveness throughout the sales process.
