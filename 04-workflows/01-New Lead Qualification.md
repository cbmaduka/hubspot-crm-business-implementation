# New Lead Qualification Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow standardizes how new catering inquiries are reviewed, qualified, and moved into the sales pipeline.

---

## Workflow Trigger

The workflow begins when a new contact submits an inquiry or is manually added to HubSpot as a prospective catering client.

---

## Entry Criteria

A contact enters this workflow when:

- A new catering inquiry is received.
- The contact has provided a valid email address or phone number.
- The inquiry relates to an upcoming event.
- The contact has not already been associated with an active deal.

---

## Workflow Actions

### 1. Create or Update Contact Record

The sales representative confirms that the following information has been recorded:

- First name
- Last name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Company name, where applicable
- Event type
- Event date
- Estimated number of guests

### 2. Send Initial Inquiry Response

The **Initial Catering Inquiry** email template is sent to acknowledge the inquiry and request any missing event details.

### 3. Review Lead Information

The sales representative reviews the inquiry to determine whether the prospective client meets the basic qualification requirements.

### 4. Create Deal

A new deal is created when sufficient event information has been provided.

The deal is associated with:

- The contact
- The relevant company, where applicable

### 5. Assign Pipeline Stage

The deal is moved to the appropriate initial sales pipeline stage, such as:

**New Inquiry**

### 6. Schedule Follow-up

A follow-up task is created for the assigned sales representative to contact the prospective client and arrange a consultation.

---

## Qualification Criteria

A lead may be considered qualified when:

- The event date is known.
- The event type is confirmed.
- The estimated guest count is available.
- The client has expressed genuine interest in receiving a quotation.
- The requested service falls within the company’s catering capacity.

---

## Expected Outcome

The workflow ensures that new inquiries are acknowledged promptly, important event information is collected, and qualified opportunities are entered into the sales pipeline for further engagement.

---

## HubSpot Free Plan Limitation

The complete workflow could not be automated within the HubSpot Free plan.

The process was therefore designed and documented for manual execution, with the intention of implementing automated triggers, task creation, and email delivery when workflow automation becomes available.

---

## Business Value

This workflow helps Gochelicious:

- Respond to inquiries consistently.
- Reduce missed sales opportunities.
- Maintain accurate CRM records.
- Improve lead response time.
- Ensure that only qualified opportunities progress through the sales pipeline.

---

## Lesson Learned

Effective CRM automation begins with a clearly documented business process. Designing the workflow before automation makes it easier to identify the required triggers, actions, properties, and responsibilities.
