# Customer Success Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow outlines the process for maintaining strong client relationships after an event has been completed, encouraging repeat business and gathering valuable customer feedback.

---

## Workflow Trigger

The workflow begins once a catering event has been successfully completed and the associated deal has been marked as **Closed Won**.

---

## Workflow Stages

### 1. Confirm Event Completion

The sales representative verifies that the event has been successfully delivered and updates the CRM accordingly.

### 2. Send Thank You Message

A thank-you email is sent to the client expressing appreciation for choosing Gochelicious and reinforcing the relationship.

### 3. Request Customer Feedback

The client is invited to share feedback on their experience to help identify areas for improvement and measure customer satisfaction.

### 4. Update Customer Record

Relevant notes, feedback, and future opportunities are recorded within the CRM to support future engagements.

### 5. Identify Future Opportunities

The sales representative reviews the account for potential repeat bookings, referrals, or additional catering services.

---

## Workflow Actions

- Mark the deal as Closed Won.
- Send a thank-you email.
- Record customer feedback.
- Update customer notes.
- Identify opportunities for future engagement.

---

## Expected Outcome

Every completed event concludes with consistent customer engagement, helping strengthen relationships and encourage repeat business.

---

## HubSpot Free Plan Limitation

While customer records and deal outcomes could be managed within HubSpot, automated post-event emails, feedback requests, and customer success workflows require workflow automation available in higher-tier HubSpot subscriptions.

---

## Business Value

This workflow helps Gochelicious to:

- Strengthen customer relationships.
- Encourage repeat bookings.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Capture valuable feedback.
- Support long-term business growth.

---

## Lesson Learned

Customer success extends beyond closing a sale. A structured post-event engagement process helps build loyalty, generate referrals, and create opportunities for future business while maintaining accurate customer records.
