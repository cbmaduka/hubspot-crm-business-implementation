# Customer Onboarding Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow outlines the process for transitioning a client from a confirmed booking to successful event preparation and delivery.

---

## Workflow Trigger

The workflow begins when a catering deal is marked as **Booked** after the required deposit has been received.

---

## Workflow Stages

### 1. Booking Confirmation

The sales representative confirms receipt of the client's deposit and updates the deal status to **Booked**.

### 2. Send Confirmation Email

A confirmation email is sent to the client summarizing the event details, catering package, and next steps.

### 3. Verify Event Details

The following information is reviewed and confirmed:

- Event date
- Event time
- Venue
- Guest count
- Selected menu
- Dietary requirements
- Special requests

### 4. Coordinate Internal Preparation

The operations team is informed of the confirmed booking to begin planning for staffing, equipment, ingredients, and logistics.

### 5. Pre-Event Check-in

The client is contacted a few days before the event to confirm that no changes have been made to the event requirements.

### 6. Event Delivery

The catering team delivers the agreed services on the scheduled event date.

---

## Workflow Actions

- Update the deal status.
- Confirm all event information.
- Notify internal teams.
- Record client communications.
- Prepare for successful event delivery.

---

## Expected Outcome

Clients receive a consistent onboarding experience, while internal teams have the information needed to prepare and deliver a successful event.

---

## HubSpot Free Plan Limitation

The onboarding process was documented for manual execution because automated emails, internal notifications, and task assignments require HubSpot workflow automation available in higher-tier plans.

---

## Business Value

This workflow helps Gochelicious to:

- Deliver a professional customer experience.
- Reduce communication gaps.
- Ensure accurate event preparation.
- Improve coordination between sales and operations.
- Increase customer confidence before the event.

---

## Lesson Learned

A structured onboarding process ensures that both customers and internal teams have clear expectations, reducing errors and improving the overall quality of service delivery.
