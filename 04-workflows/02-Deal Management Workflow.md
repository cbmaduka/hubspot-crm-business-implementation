# Deal Management Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow outlines the process for managing catering opportunities from initial qualification through to booking confirmation.

---

## Workflow Trigger

The workflow begins when a qualified lead is converted into a deal within the CRM.

---

## Workflow Stages

### 1. New Inquiry

A deal is created after a prospective client provides sufficient information about their event.

### 2. Consultation

The sales representative engages with the client to discuss event requirements, catering preferences, budget, and logistics.

### 3. Proposal Sent

A customized catering proposal is prepared and shared with the client for review.

### 4. Negotiation

The proposal is reviewed with the client, and any requested revisions are discussed before final agreement.

### 5. Deposit Pending

The client has accepted the proposal and is awaiting payment of the required deposit to secure the booking.

### 6. Booked

The required deposit has been received, and the event is confirmed.

### 7. Closed

The opportunity is marked as either:

- Closed Won – Event successfully booked.
- Closed Lost – Client declined the proposal or did not proceed.

---

## Workflow Actions

- Create and maintain an associated deal record.
- Update the sales pipeline stage as the opportunity progresses.
- Record important communications and meeting notes.
- Associate the relevant contact and company records with the deal.
- Monitor deal progress until a final outcome is reached.

---

## Expected Outcome

Every sales opportunity follows a consistent process from inquiry to closure, ensuring accurate record-keeping and better visibility into the sales pipeline.

---

## HubSpot Free Plan Limitation

While the pipeline stages were configured within HubSpot, automatic stage transitions, reminders, and notifications were not available under the Free plan and would require workflow automation in a higher-tier subscription.

---

## Business Value

This workflow enables the sales team to:

- Maintain visibility over active opportunities.
- Standardize the sales process.
- Improve forecasting and reporting.
- Reduce the risk of missed follow-ups.
- Deliver a consistent customer experience.

---

## Lesson Learned

A clearly defined deal management process improves pipeline visibility, supports accurate reporting, and creates a repeatable sales methodology that can later be enhanced through CRM automation.
