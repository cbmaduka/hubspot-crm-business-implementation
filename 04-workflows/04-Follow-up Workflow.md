# Follow-up Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow establishes a consistent process for following up with prospective and existing clients throughout the sales cycle to improve engagement and increase conversion rates.

---

## Workflow Trigger

The workflow begins after a quotation has been sent or when a scheduled follow-up activity becomes due.

---

## Workflow Stages

### 1. Initial Follow-up

The sales representative contacts the client within 2–3 business days after sending the proposal to answer questions and confirm receipt.

### 2. Ongoing Engagement

Additional follow-ups are scheduled if the client requires more time to make a decision or requests changes to the proposal.

### 3. Record Client Feedback

Any updates, questions, or requested changes are documented within the CRM to maintain a complete communication history.

### 4. Update Deal Status

The deal stage is updated based on the client's response, such as moving the opportunity to **Negotiation**, **Deposit Pending**, or **Closed Lost**.

### 5. Close Follow-up

Once the client confirms their decision, the follow-up process is concluded and the deal record is updated accordingly.

---

## Workflow Actions

- Schedule follow-up activities.
- Record communication history.
- Update deal stages.
- Respond to client enquiries.
- Maintain accurate CRM records.

---

## Expected Outcome

Every prospective client receives timely and consistent communication, reducing missed opportunities and improving the overall customer experience.

---

## HubSpot Free Plan Limitation

Automatic follow-up reminders and email sequences were not available under the HubSpot Free plan. These activities were documented for manual execution and can be automated in higher-tier subscriptions.

---

## Business Value

This workflow enables Gochelicious to:

- Improve response consistency.
- Increase customer engagement.
- Reduce lost opportunities.
- Maintain complete communication records.
- Support a structured sales process.

---

## Lesson Learned

A structured follow-up process helps ensure that potential clients remain engaged throughout the sales cycle while providing the sales team with clear visibility into every customer interaction.
